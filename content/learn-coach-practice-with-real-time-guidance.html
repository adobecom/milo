The method

How it works

Pick one exercise and share the window you're working in — nothing else is ever visible to the coach.

The coach samples your screen every few seconds and speaks up only when something's actually changed — no spam, just guidance.

Ask questions any time — before, during, or after — and submit for a per-criterion grade whenever you're ready.

The proof

This works, and it's real

This isn't a mockup — the coaching loop above is a working product you can share your screen with today.

Every exercise in the catalog started life as a real Adobe Learn tutorial. We transcribed the tutorial's own steps into a step-by-step task with a graded rubric, so the coach checks your work against the same steps Learn already teaches — not something we made up.

Learn already has other efforts underway to create exercises at scale. This coaching layer was designed to sit on top of that work, not compete with it — any exercise built through those initiatives can drop straight into the same live-coaching and grading loop.
